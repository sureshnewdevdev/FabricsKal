Common Prompt Engineering Mistakes & Solutions

Being Too Vague or General

Asking open-ended or excessively broad questions forces the AI to guess what you want, often resulting in generic or irrelevant responses.

Be Specific and Add Constraints

Provide exact details about what you want, including length, audience, and specific topics to cover.

Bad Prompt: "Write about marketing."

Good Prompt: "Write a 300-word introduction for a blog post about digital marketing strategies for small local bakeries, aimed at business owners with no marketing experience."

Lacking Context or Persona

Treating the AI like a basic search engine without providing background context leads to flat answers that don't match your specific scenario.

Assign a Role and Provide Background

Use role-playing (e.g., "Act as...") and explain why you need the information so the AI can tailor its tone and expertise.

Bad Prompt: "Explain quantum physics."

Good Prompt: "Act as a middle school science teacher. Explain quantum physics using simple, everyday analogies that a 12-year-old would easily understand."

Not Specifying the Output Format

If you don't tell the AI how you want the data presented, you might get a wall of text when you actually needed a table, a list, or code.

Explicitly State the Desired Format

Tell the AI exactly how to structure the final answer (e.g., bullet points, JSON, Markdown table, CSV).

Bad Prompt: "Compare Python and JavaScript."

Good Prompt: "Compare Python and JavaScript. Present the comparison in a Markdown table with three columns: Feature, Python, and JavaScript. Include rows for ease of use, performance, and primary use cases."

Overloading with Too Many Tasks at Once

Asking the AI to analyze data, write an essay, translate it, and format it all in one single massive prompt can cause it to skip steps or hallucinate.

Break Down Complex Tasks (Prompt Chaining)

Divide the big task into smaller, logical steps and feed them to the AI one by one.

Bad Prompt: "Read this 5-page text, summarize it, extract all the names, turn them into a table, and then write a poem about it."

Good Prompt:
Step 1: "Please summarize the main points of this text."
Step 2 (Next prompt): "Now, extract all the names of people mentioned in the text and format them into a table."

Failing to Provide Examples (Zero-Shot Failure)

When asking for a very specific style of reasoning or data extraction, relying solely on instructions can sometimes lead to inconsistent formatting.

Use Few-Shot Prompting

Provide one or two examples of the input and your desired output so the AI can mimic your exact pattern.

Bad Prompt: "Extract the fruit names from this sentence: I bought apples and oranges."

Good Prompt:
"Extract fruits from sentences.
Input: 'She ate a banana.' -> Output: Banana
Input: 'I love grapes and kiwis.' -> Output: Grapes, Kiwis
Input: 'I bought apples and oranges.' -> Output:"
